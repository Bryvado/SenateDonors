      About this map

      Itemized contributions from individuals to the principal federal campaign committees of James Talarico, John Cornyn, and Ken Paxton, placed at each contributor's reported ZIP. Receipt dates define the periods. Amendments replace superseded reports. Unitemized contributions (generally $200 or less per donor per election) are not included.

      Who led shows the first candidate's share of the two compared candidates' dollars in five steps. Places with less than a set combined amount ($250 for ZIPs and county subdivisions, $1,000 for counties and metro areas, $5,000 for districts, $10,000 for states) keep the color of whoever led but are hatched to mark them as too little to call. Total raised is the pair's combined dollars. Per 100 residents divides that by ACS 2020–2024 population and hatches places with fewer than 1,000 residents (still shaded by their rate). For these two, each color holds about a fifth of the places shown, and the legend lists the cutoffs. Only one level is colored at a time: states when nothing is open, otherwise just the open areas, with other states left plain. Contribution counts are entries, not unique donors.

      ZIP outlines are 2020 Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs), which do not match every mailing ZIP. The County, Congressional district, Metro/micro area, and County subdivision views apportion each reported ZIP's totals among the areas it overlaps, using the share of the ZIP's residential addresses in each area (all addresses for ZIPs with none) from the HUD USPS ZIP crosswalk (June 2026). Amounts and counts there are estimates. ZIPs outside the crosswalk and non-metro or unassigned portions are left out of the shading, but state totals still include them. Congressional districts are the 119th Congress districts, which the HUD crosswalk uses; Texas's 2025 mid-decade plan is not shown.

      Click a state to show it alone; Shift-, Ctrl- or ⌘-click (or “Add state”) to compare several. The panels on the left open Totals (each selected state's totals and monthly receipts), Top places (the leading areas in view by dollars, per resident, share, or average contribution), Donor map (how much of each candidate's money comes from Texas and which states give most), and Timeline (plays the race month by month on the state map). Drag panels by their title bars and resize them from the corner; they reset when you reload. Nationwide view is available for counties, districts, and metro areas.
